My Dearest Rio, 💕

Happy Birthday, my love! ❤️🎂

One of the memories I'll always carry with me is our college symposium during our third year. I was representing our department, and you were representing yours. That was the first time we really talked in person, and I still remember how our conversations started growing from there. We began talking about so many things, and little by little, we became closer. 🥹💗

And our first photo together! 📸❤️ Maybe it was just one picture taken during a college symposium, but to me, it's a little piece of our story. Whenever I think about it, I remember how unexpectedly everything started changing between us.

I also remember that night when you called me and explained what had happened. Akka was online with me at that time, too. You later called again and promised me that you wouldn't drink anymore, even though I hadn't asked you to make that promise. It was one of those moments when I realized that the things I said and felt mattered to you. 🫂

When you eventually told me about smoking, you asked for my confirmation, and I told you that I would give you my answer within one year. Time passed, and somewhere between our conversations, memories, and all the little things we shared, I started understanding my own heart better.

I decided on 21.04.2026 that I wanted to tell you my feelings on my birthday. And when I finally told you that I loved you, it felt like we were stepping into a new chapter of our story. ❤️🥹

When I look back now, I realize how many little moments brought us here. The conversations, the first time we talked in person, our first photo, the calls, and the feelings that slowly became clearer to me — every part has its own place in my heart.

Thank you for sharing your stories with me, for letting me know the real you, and for giving me memories that I can look back on with a smile. I hope we continue to understand each other, communicate honestly, support each other's dreams, and grow together. I want our relationship to be a place where we both feel loved, respected, and safe enough to be ourselves. 💕

On your birthday, I wish you all the happiness you deserve, success in everything you work towards, good health, peace of mind, and the courage to follow your dreams. I hope this new year of your life brings you countless reasons to smile.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be part of many more of those smiles. I want more photos with you, more long conversations, more bike rides, more silly fights that end in laughter, more celebrations, and more ordinary days that become beautiful simply because we shared them. 😂❤️

No matter how our story began, I'm happy about the chapter we're writing now. Thank you for becoming such a special part of my life. I'll always treasure the memories we've made and look forward to the ones still waiting for us.

Happy Birthday once again, my favourite human, my Rio. I hope you feel loved today, tomorrow, and on all the ordinary days in between. I love you more than I can properly explain. 🫂💗

Forever yours,
Your Ashwini ❤️
